Dropshipping Profit Maths: Where the Money in a Rs 699 Order Actually Goes

Updated October 2026 · Money Moves India

Many people see a Rs 699 selling price and think the profit is huge. Then the costs show up. This guide follows one order line by line so you know your break-even ad cost before you spend a rupee on ads.

One delivered order

ItemAmount
Selling priceRs 699
Product cost from supplierRs 250
ShippingRs 70
Payment or COD handlingRs 14
PackagingRs 15
Left before adsRs 350

So on a clean delivered order you keep Rs 350 before advertising. That looks fine, but returns change the picture.

Add returns: 100 orders placed

Suppose 20 of every 100 orders are returned (this is an example figure, and COD returns can be high). You pay return shipping of Rs 140 on those, and you lose the ad money spent to get them.

LineAmount
Delivered orders80
Revenue (80 × Rs 699)Rs 55,920
Product cost (80 × Rs 250)Rs 20,000
Shipping (80 × Rs 70)Rs 5,600
Handling and packaging (80 × Rs 29)Rs 2,320
Return shipping (20 × Rs 140)Rs 2,800
Profit before adsRs 25,200

Your break-even ad cost

Rs 25,200 across 100 placed orders is Rs 252 per placed order. If you pay more than Rs 252 in ads to get one order, you lose money. If you pay Rs 150, you keep about Rs 102 per order. Check this number before you scale ads.

Checklist before you start

  • Order a sample to check quality and delivery time.
  • Ask the supplier about their return process.
  • Use your own return rate, not a guess.
  • Test with a small ad budget and track cost per order.

All figures are examples for learning. Your costs will differ, and no income is guaranteed.
