Build a Student Expense Tracker in Google Sheets in 20 Minutes

Updated October 2026 · Money Moves India

You do not need a paid app to track your spending. A free Google Sheet can do it in about 20 minutes. By the end you will have a list to type expenses into and a summary that adds them up by category.

Step 1: Make the expense sheet

  1. Open sheets.google.com and click Blank.
  2. Rename the first tab to Expenses.
  3. In row 1 type: A1 Date, B1 Item, C1 Category, D1 Amount.
  4. Select row 1 and click Bold.

Step 2: Add the category dropdown

  1. Select cells C2:C500.
  2. Click Data, then Data validation, then Add rule.
  3. Under Criteria choose Dropdown.
  4. Add the items: Food, Travel, Books, Recharge, Fun, Other.
  5. Click Done.

Step 3: Make the summary tab

  1. Click the + at the bottom to add a new tab and name it Summary.
  2. Type the six categories in A2 to A7.
  3. In B2 type =SUMIF(Expenses!C:C, A2, Expenses!D:D) and press Enter.
  4. Drag the small square at the corner of B2 down to B7.
  5. In A8 type Total and in B8 type =SUM(B2:B7).

Tips to keep using it

  • Add each expense the same day, it takes 10 seconds.
  • Look at the Summary on the 1st of every month.
  • Pick one category to cut by Rs 200 next month.
