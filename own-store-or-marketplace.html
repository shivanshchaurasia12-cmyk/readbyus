Own Store or Marketplace? A Decision Guide for Small Sellers in India

Updated October 2026 · Money Moves India

New sellers often ask: should I open my own store or sell on a marketplace like Meesho or Flipkart? They solve different problems. A marketplace gives you customers. An own store gives you control.

Side-by-side

PointMarketplaceOwn store
TrafficCustomers already visitYou must bring every visitor
FeesCommission and shipping deductionsPayment fee plus monthly platform costs
Pricing controlLimited, many sellers competeFull control
Customer dataMostly with the platformYou own the list
Setup effortLowHigher

Worked example

A product sells for Rs 600. It costs you Rs 300 and shipping is Rs 60. These fee numbers are assumptions for learning, so replace them with your real ones.

LineMarketplaceOwn store
Selling priceRs 600Rs 600
Product costRs 300Rs 300
ShippingRs 60Rs 60
Marketplace fees (15%)Rs 90-
Payment fee (2%)-Rs 12
Ad cost per order-Rs 150
Profit per orderRs 150Rs 78

Now add a fixed cost of Rs 2,000 a month for the store and apps. At 100 orders that is Rs 20 per order, so profit falls to about Rs 58. At 500 orders it is only Rs 4 per order, so profit rises to about Rs 74. The own store gets better as volume and repeat customers grow.

How to decide

  • Starting out with little money: begin on a marketplace and learn what sells.
  • Already have regular customers or a social audience: an own store makes sense.
  • Many sellers do both: marketplace for sales, own store for repeat buyers.

Fees and plans change often. Check the current charges on each platform before you decide.
