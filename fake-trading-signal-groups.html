8 Warning Signs of a Fake Trading Signal Group

Updated October 2026 · Money Moves India

Telegram and WhatsApp groups that sell trading 'signals' are everywhere. Some are honest, many are not. Use these eight warning signs to protect your money.

The eight warning signs

Warning signWhat to check
1. Guaranteed returnsNo one can guarantee profit in a market. Walk away.
2. Only winning screenshotsScreenshots are easy to edit. Ask for full, verified records.
3. Pressure to act now'Only 5 seats left' is a sales trick, not a trading reason.
4. Payment to a personal accountReal businesses give proper invoices and company accounts.
5. Admin not registeredInvestment advice in India needs SEBI registration. Search the name on sebi.gov.in.
6. 'Insider' or 'operator' tipsClaims of inside information are a red flag, and trading on it is illegal.
7. Push to deposit into an unknown appCheck the broker with the steps in our legality checklist first.
8. Trouble withdrawingExtra fees or delays to take out your own money mean stop.

Worked example: even good signals can lose

A group charges Rs 3,000 a month. Over 20 trades, the signals win 11 and lose 9, and each trade wins or loses Rs 500. That sounds good, but see the result.

LineAmount
Wins (11 × Rs 500)+Rs 5,500
Losses (9 × Rs 500)-Rs 4,500
Trading result+Rs 1,000
Group fee-Rs 3,000
Net-Rs 2,000

The fee alone can turn a small edge into a loss. Add slippage and brokerage and it gets worse.

If you already paid

  • Stop sending any more money, even for 'release fees'.
  • Save chats, screenshots and payment proofs.
  • Report at cybercrime.gov.in or call the helpline 1930 as soon as possible.
  • Tell your bank about the transaction.
