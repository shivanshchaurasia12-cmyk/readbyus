Revenge Trading: How One Loss Turns Into Four

Updated October 2026 · Money Moves India

Revenge trading means entering a new trade quickly, and usually bigger, to win back what you just lost. It feels like fixing the problem. It usually makes it worse.

The pattern

It starts with a normal loss. You feel angry or want it back. You skip your rules, double the size and enter without a clear reason. If that loses too, you double again.

Worked example: Rs 20,000 account

Your plan risks Rs 400 per trade. Compare four losses in a row under the plan and under revenge trading, where the size doubles after each loss.

TradeSticking to the planRevenge trading
Loss 1Rs 400, balance 19,600Rs 400, balance 19,600
Loss 2Rs 400, balance 19,200Rs 800, balance 18,800
Loss 3Rs 400, balance 18,800Rs 1,600, balance 17,200
Loss 4Rs 400, balance 18,400Rs 3,200, balance 14,000

With the plan you lost Rs 1,600, which is 8% of the account. With revenge trading you lost Rs 6,000, which is 30%. To get back to Rs 20,000 from Rs 14,000 you now need a gain of about 43%. From Rs 18,400 you only need about 8.7%. Big losses are very hard to recover.

How to stop it

  • Set a daily loss limit, such as 2 losing trades, then close the screen.
  • Never increase size after a loss.
  • Write down the reason for every trade before you enter.
  • Take a 30-minute break after any loss that makes you angry.
